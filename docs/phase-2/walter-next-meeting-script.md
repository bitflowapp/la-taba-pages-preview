# Walter next meeting script

## Open the demo

Open the published URL on the phone or laptop:

https://bitflowapp.github.io/la-taba-pages-preview/

Then say:

- This is the first version of a La Taba channel propio de pedidos.
- The client enters, chooses products, and sends the order by WhatsApp already ordered.
- The goal is not to replace every other channel on day one.

## How to explain it

Use a short explanation:

- The client sees products.
- The client builds the order.
- The business receives it ordered.
- The business can prepare, ship, or mark the order as delivered.

## Questions to ask

- What products should be in the first real catalog?
- Which combos do you want to push first?
- Which prices are the real ones?
- Which zones do you deliver to today?
- What delivery fee do you use?
- What minimum order do you want?
- What payment methods should appear first?
- How do you manage WhatsApp today?
- What is the most annoying part of the current process?

## What to ask for next

- Real product list.
- Real prices.
- Product photos.
- Combo photos.
- Delivery zones.
- Delivery fee rules.
- Stock expectations.
- Any notes about how the team prepares orders.

## How to close without pressure

Say something like:

- This is a good base for a first real version.
- We can start with a small catalog and the delivery zones you actually use.
- The next step is to prepare the real product data and photos.
- After that we can decide if it is worth moving to a real backend.

## How to ask for a first real version

Keep it simple:

- If you want, we can turn this into a first real version with your products, prices, photos and delivery zones.
- We can start small and improve after the first live use.
- The important thing is to keep the WhatsApp flow working and make the order clearer.
